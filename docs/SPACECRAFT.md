# Spacecraft racer direction

The user selected sleek spacecraft: flowing bodies, luminous engines and retractable hydrofoils. Vehicles must read as spacecraft immediately. The original hydroplane has been replaced by three distinct spacecraft modules.

Three implemented silhouettes, retaining existing handling roles:
- Kestrel: balanced interceptor, arrowhead blended body, swept shoulders, twin recessed engines.
- Albatross: speed craft, stretched fuselage and swept engine nacelles.
- Manta: compact, broad crescent wing-body and small vectoring nozzles for agility.

Shared language: ivory composite, graphite underside, restrained team-color panels, smoked glass and cyan engine light. Fine panel seams, machined nozzle recesses and articulated foils provide detail. Foils deploy while skimming and retract during jumps; engine luminosity and exhaust length respond to boost.

Original Atlas concept images guided the silhouettes. Generated Three.js geometry follows the recipe, with static body sections batched and moving foils retained as named hierarchies. Existing campaign clearances and handling roles remain the basis of the game.
