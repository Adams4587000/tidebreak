# Six crews of the Drowned Frontier

The previous three enclosed spacecraft are superseded by exposed rider–machine pairs. All six load through canonical `ASSET` with hierarchy preservation. Static body, rider and left/right foil subtrees are batched separately by `bakeStatic`. Exhaust plumes retain their own pivots. During racing the rider leans into steering, the hull responds to waves, foils fold in jumps and deploy under speed, and propulsion reacts to boost.

| Pair | Structural silhouette | Rider identity | Unlock |
|---|---|---|---|
| Rook / Kestrel | Forked salvage bow, external protection pipes, twin compact engines | Aviator brow, narrow gold visor, patched utility armor | Starter |
| Vesper / Albatross | Long needle spine, narrow outriggers, dorsal blade | Streamlined helmet crest, bone armor, flight harness | One race finish |
| Moss / Manta | Wide crescent deck, shallow hull, segmented trailing edges | Respirator filters, olive armor, life-support canisters | Twelve collected perks in finished races |
| Cinder / Brimstone | Large parallel turbine pods, exposed manifold pipes | Angular furnace helmet, red visor, layered heat armor | Two podiums |
| Echo / Spectre | Offset intake and engine assembly, side antenna | Hood, blue visor, communications backpack | Four race finishes |
| Marshal / Ironclad | Broad armored twin hulls, bridging chassis and pistons | Square industrial helmet, heavy shoulder and shin plates | One win |

Every model includes actual geometry for saddle, foot supports, control grips, display, service hatches, cooling grills, seam rails, fasteners, hoses, turbine blades, armor overlaps, gloves and boots. The helmet and armor layouts differ as well as the hulls. The dock offers whole-machine and closer rider views. Locked pairs can be inspected, but their start control remains disabled.

Geometry source: `tools/dystopia-craft.template.js`, regenerated by `python3 tools/make-dystopia-craft.py`. Models are roughly 18–22k triangles each before runtime batching. Runtime surfaces combine Atlas chipped-paint albedo, PATINA fine normal/roughness and the existing Atlas carbon composite set. Sources are recorded in CREDITS.md.

This is constructor-authored geometry with six hull architectures and iterative multi-angle/in-game review. It is not a claim that three fully independent candidates were produced for every individual asset or that a blind comparison established parity with the reference images. The earlier closed-canopy spacecraft reference identifiers remain in git history and the references directory for provenance.
